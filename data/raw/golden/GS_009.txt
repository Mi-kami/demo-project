CUSTOMER: It was issued in 2017. But I did

been using it everywhere — for M-Pesa 
registration, for everything. Nobody has 
ever told me it was expired.

AGENT: I completely understand your 
confusion, Wanjiru. Kenyan national IDs 
issued before 2019 do not technically 
expire in the traditional sense, however 
our system flags IDs that are more than 
eight years old for manual review because 
the photograph and details may no longer 
accurately represent the account holder. 
It is a compliance requirement on our end 
rather than a government expiry rule.

CUSTOMER: So the ID itself is not expired 
but your system is rejecting it because 
it is old. That is a bit confusing. What 
am I supposed to do then? I cannot just 
get a new ID overnight.

AGENT: I understand, and I apologise for 
the inconvenience. You have two options. 
The first option is to visit a Huduma 
Centre to apply for an updated national 
ID — the process typically takes two to 
three weeks. Once you have the new ID 
you can resubmit through the app and 
verification should go through cleanly.

CUSTOMER: Two to three weeks. My account 
is restricted right now and I need to 
make transactions. Is there a faster 
option?

AGENT: Yes — the second option is to 
submit an alternative government issued 
document in place of the national ID. 
A valid Kenyan passport or a KRA PIN 
certificate combined with a recent 
utility bill dated within the last 
three months would be accepted for 
verification purposes.

CUSTOMER: I do not have a passport. 
But I have my KRA PIN. What counts 
as a recent utility bill — does a 
Safaricom bill work?

AGENT: Yes, a Safaricom statement 
showing your name and current address 
dated within the last three months 
is acceptable. You would need to 
submit both the KRA PIN certificate 
and the Safaricom statement together 
through the app under the document 
submission section. Once both are 
uploaded our verification team 
will review within forty eight hours.

CUSTOMER: Okay. And my account 
will stay restricted until they 
finish reviewing?

AGENT: Unfortunately yes — your 
account will remain on restricted 
status until verification is 
complete. However once the 
documents are approved the 
restriction will be lifted 
automatically and you will 
receive an SMS confirmation.
